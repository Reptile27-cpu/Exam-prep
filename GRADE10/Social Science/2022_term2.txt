GRADE 10 SOCIAL SCIENCE - TERM 2 2022 EXAMINATION
Papua New Guinea National Examination Board

TIME ALLOWED: 2 hours
TOTAL MARKS: 80

SECTION A: HISTORY (30 MARKS)

1. (10 marks) Papua New Guinea Independence
   a) When did Papua New Guinea gain independence?
   b) Who was the first Prime Minister?
   c) Name three challenges PNG faced after independence
   d) Explain the significance of September 16th
   e) How did Australia influence PNG's development?

2. (10 marks) World War II in the Pacific
   a) When did WWII begin in the Pacific?
   b) Explain the significance of the Kokoda Track campaign
   c) How did PNG contribute to the Allied victory?
   d) Name three battle sites in PNG
   e) What were the long-term effects of WWII on PNG?

3. (10 marks) Traditional PNG Society
   a) Describe the traditional land ownership system
   b) Explain the importance of the "wantok" system
   c) How were disputes traditionally resolved?
   d) What role did chiefs play in traditional society?
   e) How has Christianity influenced traditional customs?

SECTION B: GEOGRAPHY (30 MARKS)

4. (10 marks) Physical Geography of PNG
   a) Name the five main regions of PNG
   b) Describe the climate of the coastal regions
   c) Explain the formation of the Highlands
   d) Name three major rivers of PNG
   e) Why is PNG so mountainous?

5. (10 marks) Economic Activities
   a) What are the main agricultural exports of PNG?
   b) Explain the importance of mining to PNG's economy
   c) Describe the fishing industry in PNG
   d) How does tourism contribute to the economy?
   e) What challenges does PNG face in economic development?

6. (10 marks) Population and Settlement
   a) What is the current population of PNG (approximately)?
   b) Explain why most people live in the Highlands
   c) Describe urbanization in PNG
   d) What problems does rapid urban growth cause?
   e) How can rural development be improved?

SECTION C: CIVICS (20 MARKS)

7. (10 marks) Government and Democracy
   a) What type of government does PNG have?
   b) Describe the role of the Prime Minister
   c) Explain the function of Parliament
   d) How are members of Parliament elected?
   e) What is the importance of voting in a democracy?

8. (10 marks) Rights and Responsibilities
   a) List five basic human rights in PNG
   b) What are the responsibilities of a PNG citizen?
   c) Explain the importance of the rule of law
   d) How does the Constitution protect citizens?
   e) What should you do if your rights are violated?

END OF EXAMINATION

ANSWER GUIDE:
Section A: Focus on accurate dates, names, and historical understanding
Section B: Credit for geographical knowledge and economic awareness
Section C: Emphasis on understanding of democratic principles and civic responsibility
