    45, 52, 48, 65, 72, 58, 61, 55, 49, 67,

    57, 63, 69, 46, 51, 64, 73, 56, 60, 74,

    b) Draw a histogram for the data
    c) Calculate the mean mark
    d) Find the median mark
    e) Determine the mode
    f) Calculate the standard deviation

13. (15 marks) Trigonometry
    From the top of a 50m tall building, the angle of depression of a car on the ground is 30°.
