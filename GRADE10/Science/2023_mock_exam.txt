GRADE 10 SCIENCE - 2023 MOCK EXAMINATION
Papua New Guinea National Examination Board

TIME ALLOWED: 2 hours 30 minutes
TOTAL MARKS: 100

SECTION A: PHYSICS (35 MARKS)

1. (5 marks) Define the following terms:
   a) Force
   b) Acceleration
   c) Momentum
   d) Work
   e) Power

2. (10 marks) A car of mass 1200kg accelerates from rest to 25m/s in 8 seconds.
   a) Calculate the acceleration
   b) Find the force applied
   c) Determine the momentum at 25m/s
   d) Calculate the kinetic energy
   e) If the car continues at this speed for 2 minutes, how far does it travel?

3. (10 marks) Electricity
   a) Draw the circuit diagram for a simple series circuit with a battery, switch, and two resistors
   b) If the battery is 12V and the resistors are 4Ω and 6Ω, calculate:
      i) Total resistance
      ii) Current flowing
      iii) Voltage across each resistor
   c) Explain why parallel circuits are used in home wiring

4. (10 marks) Waves and Sound
   a) Define wavelength, frequency, and amplitude
   b) Calculate the frequency of a wave with wavelength 0.5m traveling at 340m/s
   c) Explain how the human ear detects sound
   d) Why can't we hear in space?

SECTION B: CHEMISTRY (35 MARKS)

5. (8 marks) Atomic Structure
   a) Draw the atomic structure of sodium (Na = 23)
   b) Explain why sodium is highly reactive
   c) Write the chemical equation for sodium reacting with water
   d) What type of bond forms in NaCl?

6. (12 marks) Acids and Bases
   a) Define acid and base according to:
      i) Arrhenius theory
      ii) Bronsted-Lowry theory
   b) Classify the following as acidic, basic, or neutral:
      i) HCl  ii) NaOH  iii) NaCl  iv) Water
   c) Calculate the pH of a solution with [H+] = 1×10⁻³ mol/L
   d) Write the neutralization reaction between H₂SO₄ and NaOH

7. (10 marks) Carbon Compounds
   a) What is a hydrocarbon?
   b) Draw and name the isomers of C₄H₁₀
   c) Explain why alkanes are less reactive than alkenes
   d) Write the balanced equation for complete combustion of ethane

8. (5 marks) Chemical Reactions
   Write balanced equations for:
   a) Zinc + Hydrochloric acid →
   b) Copper(II) oxide + Hydrogen →
   c) Sodium carbonate + Hydrochloric acid →
   d) Iron + Oxygen →
   e) Calcium carbonate → (on heating)

SECTION C: BIOLOGY (30 MARKS)

9. (10 marks) Cells and Tissues
   a) Draw and label a plant cell
   b) State three differences between plant and animal cells
   c) Explain the function of mitochondria
   d) What is the importance of cell division?

10. (10 marks) Human Nutrition
   a) Draw the human digestive system
   b) Explain the function of:
      i) Stomach
      ii) Small intestine
      iii) Liver
   c) Why is proper nutrition important for teenagers?

11. (10 marks) Ecosystems
   a) Define ecosystem and give an example
   b) Draw a simple food chain for a PNG forest ecosystem
   c) Explain the importance of biodiversity
   d) How does deforestation affect the ecosystem?

END OF EXAMINATION

MARKING SCHEME:
Section A: Full marks for correct definitions and calculations
Section B: Emphasis on correct chemical equations and understanding
Section C: Credit for accurate diagrams and biological concepts
